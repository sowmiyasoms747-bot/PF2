UX RESEARCH & METHODOLOGY

The framework behind every design decision

Rooted in cognitive empathy, iterative prototyping, and continuous user validation.

STAGE 01

Discover

Uncovering unarticulated user frustrations through generative interviews and contextual inquiry.

  • 1-on-1 User Interviews
  • 14-Day Diary Studies
  • Competitive Benchmarking
STAGE 02

Synthesize

Transforming messy qualitative notes into clear mental models, personas, and task journeys.

  • Affinity Mapping
  • Empathy & Persona Profiles
  • How-Might-We Statements
STAGE 03

Wireframe

Mapping architectural flows, navigation hierarchy, and low-fidelity paper blueprints.

  • Paper Wireframe Sketches
  • Information Architecture
  • Clickable Greyscale Flows
STAGE 04

Prototype

Building dynamic interactive prototypes with realistic variables, states, and micro-interactions.

  • Figma Component Variants
  • Coded Prototypes (JS/CSS)
  • Usability Benchmarking
STAGE 05

Deliver

Packaging design tokens, WCAG specs, and engineering handoff documentation for frictionless shipping.

  • Design System Tokens
  • Accessibility Audits
  • Post-Launch Analytics

Synthesized Affinity Board (Park Share Discovery)

Click filters to view categorized user quotes and insights from 24 interview sessions.

Pain Point • Commuter

“I hate arriving at a garage only to discover clearance is under 6'2" or all spots are reserved. It doubles my stress before client meetings.”

User Need • Commuter

“Show me exact walking distance in minutes from the parking spot to my destination pin, not just straight-line bird flight distance.”

Opportunity • Business

“Host driveway monetization: If residential hosts can set an automated calendar for work hours (9 AM - 5 PM), supply jumps by 64%.”

Pain Point • Host

“I worry about unauthorized cars overstaying their booked window. I need instant host notification and an overstay fee mechanism.”

User Need • Accessibility

“Filter by EV charging stations and ground-level wheelchair accessible bays right on the main map filter drawer.”

Opportunity • UX Flow

“1-Tap Apple Pay / Google Pay checkout: Drivers booking on the move cannot manually type 16 credit card digits safely.”

Pain Point • Trust

“Photos of the parking entrance are crucial. If the alley looks sketchy or narrow, I cancel immediately.”

User Need • Real-Time

“Turn-by-turn navigation link straight into Google Maps or Waze once reservation is confirmed with single tap.”
