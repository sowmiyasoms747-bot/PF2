Synthesized Affinity Board (Park Share Discovery)

Click filters to view categorized user quotes and insights from 24 interview sessions.

Pain Point • Commuter

“I hate arriving at a garage only to discover clearance is under 6'2" or all spots are reserved. It doubles my stress before client meetings.”

User Need • Commuter

“Show me exact walking distance in minutes from the parking spot to my destination pin, not just straight-line bird flight distance.”

Opportunity • Business

“Host driveway monetization: If residential hosts can set an automated calendar for work hours (9 AM - 5 PM), supply jumps by 64%.”

Pain Point • Host

“I worry about unauthorized cars overstaying their booked window. I need instant host notification and an overstay fee mechanism.”

User Need • Accessibility

“Filter by EV charging stations and ground-level wheelchair accessible bays right on the main map filter drawer.”

Opportunity • UX Flow

“1-Tap Apple Pay / Google Pay checkout: Drivers booking on the move cannot manually type 16 credit card digits safely.”

Pain Point • Trust

“Photos of the parking entrance are crucial. If the alley looks sketchy or narrow, I cancel immediately.”

User Need • Real-Time

“Turn-by-turn navigation link straight into Google Maps or Waze once reservation is confirmed with single tap.”
